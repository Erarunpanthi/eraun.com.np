ABOUT CIVPREPMASTER

Built for a more focused study routine.

CivPrepMaster’s PSC Civil Prep hub is an independent study aid for civil engineering candidates preparing for Nepal Public Service Commission and Lok Sewa examinations. It brings a practical topic organiser, revision prompts, sample questions, and a flexible weekly plan into one simple place.
