Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy describes the information practices of the PSC Civil Prep study pages on eraun.com.np. The site is a static educational resource; it does not require an account or sign-in.

Study-plan progress

If you tick the four-week study-plan boxes, the page saves those choices in your browser’s local storage so they remain after you return. This information stays in that browser on that device; the page does not send the checklist to a study account or a server. You can clear it using your browser’s site-data settings. Quiz answers and search terms are not saved by the study hub.

Contacting the site

The contact page opens your email application using a mail link. If you choose to send a message, its contents and any contact details you include are handled by your email provider and the site recipient. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information.

Technical logs and external links

The web host may process standard technical request information, such as IP address, browser type, and requested page, to deliver and protect the site. Links to the Public Service Commission or other external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices; review those policies separately.

Cookies and analytics

The PSC Civil Prep pages do not use an account system or intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser may use local storage for the study-plan checklist described above. Other pages or services you visit may behave differently.

Changes and questions

This notice may be updated when the site’s functionality changes. For questions about this policy, contact info@arunpanthi.com.np.

Important: CivPrepMaster is an independent study aid and is not the official Public Service Commission of Nepal website.
